## Post-Task Workflow Retrospective

Based on your completed work, provide a **very brief, evidence-based retrospective**:

1. **Workflow effectiveness:** What worked well, and what caused friction (instructions, roles, constraints, handoffs, validation)?
2. **Efficiency:** Where did you waste the most time, tokens, or reasoning effort due to ambiguity, missing context, or unnecessary steps?
3. **Root causes:** Which issues could have been prevented through clearer instructions or a better workflow?
4. **Improvements:** Suggest the **1–2 highest-impact changes** to the original prompt, workflow, or skills (maximum 2 sentences total).

**Rules:** Be specific, actionable, and concise. Focus on recurring or preventable problems, not generic observations. Do not invent token measurements. Keep the entire response under 120 words. Do not modify any files.
